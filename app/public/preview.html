Alarm sounds

All five, loudest-3s first. Each is 20 s, 16-bit mono PCM at 22.05 kHz, peaking at −0.3 dBFS.

Laptop speakers will mislead you. These were tuned for a phone speaker, which barely reproduces anything below 500 Hz. The only judgement that counts is listening on the iPhone, at the volume you would actually sleep at, from across the room.
